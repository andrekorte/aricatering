About

Our story

Welcome to Ari

Ari was born from my heart and my love for sharing food, stories and smiles. I grew up in Thailand, surrounded by a big, loving family where food brought us all together. Every meal was a celebration — of flavour, of laughter, of being with the people you love.

I moved to Australia 24 years ago, and this country quickly became my second home. Along the way I found so much to love, but I never lost my passion for the bold, fresh, colourful flavours of Thai street food. That passion is what inspired Ari.

Catering felt like the natural next step. The best moments in Thailand happen around a table that’s too full, with everyone reaching across each other. That’s what we want to bring into your office — not a stack of identical lunch boxes, but a proper spread that gets people talking.

I want every bite to take you on a little trip to Thailand — to make you feel like you’re dancing through a busy market street, surrounded by delicious smells and warm smiles.

Whether your team is trying Thai food for the first time or has loved it for years, you’re always welcome here. Thanks for stopping by. I can’t wait to cook for you.

— Yada

The name

Why “Ari”?

Ari isn’t just a name — it’s a feeling. In Bangkok, Ari is a trendy, vibrant suburb known for its cool cafés, creative energy and, most of all, amazing street food. It’s the kind of place where you can grab something delicious any time of day or night.

Locals love it for its laid-back vibe, buzzing food stalls, and the way it brings people together. Whether you’re on a quick lunch break, catching up with friends, or craving a late-night snack, Ari is the place to be.

That’s exactly the spirit we bring to catering. Fast, fresh, full of flavour, and made to share. To us, real Thai food isn’t just about spice or ingredients — it’s about community. It’s about dishes that bring people together, start conversations and make memories.
