Ordering

How to order and pay

  1. Email your brief

    Date, numbers, address, package and any dietary requirements to arithaistreetfood@gmail.com.

  2. We confirm and invoice

    A minimum 25% deposit confirms the order. Credit card payment is available, and we can invoice a business.

  3. Changes and cancellations

    Changes can be made up to 24 hours before. Cancellations are not refunded — the amount paid becomes credit towards a future order.

Good to know

Catering questions we get asked

How many people do you cater for?

Our minimum catering order is 10 people. We cater for office lunches, small groups and larger orders of approximately 40 to 50 people. For anything bigger, email us and we will review the options with you.

How far in advance should I order?

Please place your catering order at least 48 hours in advance.

What are your delivery hours?

Catering delivery is available from 9.00am to 6.00pm.

Do you offer free delivery?

Yes. Orders for 10 people or more receive free delivery within 5 km of the Brisbane CBD.

Can you deliver outside the free-delivery area?

Yes. Delivery outside the free-delivery area can be arranged. Delivery fees start from $15 and vary depending on distance and order size.

Can I pick up my catering order?

Yes. Pick-up is available from Ari - Thai Street Food in Brisbane City.

Can I choose a delivery time?

Yes. Tell us what time your meeting or event starts. We aim to deliver about 30 minutes beforehand so the food arrives fresh and ready to serve.

Where will you deliver within an office building?

We can deliver to an agreed location such as reception, a meeting area or another nominated point, subject to building access.

What type of food do you offer for catering?

Our catering is Thai rice meals. Depending on the package you choose, guests have one or two food choices.

What dishes are available?

Popular choices include Massaman Chicken, Green Curry Chicken, Pad Kra Pao with chicken or pork, fried rice and Tom Yum fried rice. Menu availability may vary.

Can I add a fried egg?

Yes. A fried egg can be added where available.

Do you have vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options?

Yes. Vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free choices are available. Please tell us your dietary requirements when you order.

How do you handle food allergies?

We can provide ingredient information for menu items so you can check anything relevant to your allergies or dietary needs. Our kitchen prepares all dishes in one place using shared equipment, so we cannot guarantee that any dish is free of traces of an allergen. Please tell us about any allergies and confirm them with our staff when you order.

Can meals be individually packed?

Yes. You can choose individual meal packaging for each guest.

Can the food be supplied for sharing?

Yes. You can also choose shared-style catering for your group.

Can we mix different meal choices in one order?

Yes. Menu selections can be arranged according to the package and the group’s requirements.

Do you offer desserts and drinks?

Yes. You can choose food only, food and dessert, or food, dessert and a drink.

How do I order and pay?

Email us at arithaistreetfood@gmail.com with your date, numbers and requirements. We confirm the order and issue an invoice. Credit card payment is available.

Can you provide an invoice for a business?

Yes. We can provide an invoice for corporate and business orders.

Are your prices inclusive of GST?

No. The listed catering prices are exclusive of GST.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A minimum 25% deposit is required to confirm the catering order.

Can I change my order after booking?

Yes. Changes can be made up to 24 hours before the scheduled order.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations are non-refundable. The amount paid will be provided as credit for a future order, subject to our catering terms.

Do you cater for corporate events?

Yes. We cater for office lunches, meetings, team events and corporate functions.

Do you cater for Christmas parties?

Yes. We cater for Christmas and end-of-year celebrations.

Do you cater for weddings and private events?

Yes. We cater for weddings and small private events. Menu and service options are discussed according to the event, so email us with the details.

Can you cater for events of up to 40 to 50 people?

Yes. We can accommodate catering orders of approximately 40 to 50 people. For larger or more complex events, email us to discuss your requirements.
