Good to know

Questions we get asked

How much notice do you need?

24 hours for the Street Lunch package, and 48 hours for Banquet and Street Feast so we can prep the entrées and desserts. If you’re in a bind, call us — we can often make same-day work for smaller groups.

What is the minimum order?

10 guests for Street Lunch, 15 for Ari Banquet and 20 for Street Feast. Below 10 people you’re usually better off ordering direct from the restaurant — happy to point you there.

Do you deliver outside the CBD?

Delivery into Brisbane CBD and the inner suburbs is included in the per-person price. Further out we’ll quote the delivery separately — just put the address on the enquiry form.

How do you handle allergies?

Tell us on the enquiry form and we’ll build the menu around it. Dietary dishes are cooked separately and labelled clearly on delivery. Please note our kitchen handles nuts, shellfish, gluten, soy and sesame, so we can’t guarantee a dish is free of traces.

Can we get an invoice for the finance team?

Yes. We invoice with an ABN and can quote against a purchase order number. Add the PO number to the notes field and it will appear on the invoice.

What if our numbers change?

Final numbers are due 48 hours before delivery. Small increases after that are usually fine — call and we’ll do what we can.
