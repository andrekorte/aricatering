How it works

Three steps, one business day

  1. Send us the brief

    Date, headcount, delivery address and any dietary requirements. Two minutes on the quote form.

  2. We send a menu and a fixed quote

    Back within one business day with a menu built around your group and an all-inclusive price. Adjust it as much as you like.

  3. We deliver hot and set up

    We arrive ahead of your start time, set the buffet, label the dietary dishes and get out of your way.

Dietary requirements

Thai food is genuinely good at this. Our vegan and gluten-free dishes are the same dishes — same wok, same flavour — not an afterthought plated separately.
