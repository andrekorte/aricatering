How it works

From email to lunch

  1. Email us the details

    Date, numbers, delivery address and any dietary requirements.

  2. We confirm and invoice

    A 25% deposit confirms the order. Credit card payment is available.

  3. We deliver, ready to serve

    About 30 minutes before you start, to reception or wherever you nominate.

Dietary requirements

Vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free

  • Vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free choices available
  • Tell us your requirements when you order and we build the order around them
  • Ingredient information available for any dish
  • Menu choices can be mixed within one order

Our kitchen prepares all dishes in one place using shared equipment, so we cannot guarantee that any dish is free of traces of an allergen. Please confirm any allergies and dietary requirements with our staff when you order.
